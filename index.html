From cells to living systems

Select a stage to explore

Stem cells

Stem-cell-derived cultures are a starting point in this conceptual map of models and measurements.

Brain organoid

Brain and stem-cell-derived 3D organoids provide models of tissue-level architecture and activity.

Electrophysiology

Patch-clamp and extracellular recordings measure ionic currents and action potentials. Our CF research examines neuronal activity and responses to CFTR modulators.

Sensory neuronal ganglia

Sensory neurons and their extending processes link the neuronal models shown in this conceptual research map.

Enteric nervous system

The map connects neuronal measurements with the nervous system of the gut. Our research links cellular findings with intestinal motility.

Gut-segment contraction

Tissue recordings measure contractile responses. Gut-segment contraction is one of the functional measurements highlighted in this research map.

Intestinal organoid

Intestinal organoids form a complementary model alongside tissue-level gut measurements. Our CF research includes organoid and gut-on-a-chip approaches.
